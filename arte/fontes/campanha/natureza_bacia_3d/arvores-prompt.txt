Production MODELING REFERENCE BOARD for a polished stylized fantasy RPG in 3D, warm wooden watermill art direction. Three trees: mature oak, slender young oak, natural pine. Orthographic elevated FRONT camera without lateral yaw. The oak must have an expressive gently curved branching trunk, visible root flare, asymmetric spreading canopy with irregular lobed silhouette, foliage in overlapping branch fans with individual tapered leaves and clear dark interior gaps, naturally lit leaf groups. NO balls or spheres for the canopy, NO broccoli clusters, NO noisy confetti, NO triangular low-poly facets, NO massive straight cylinder trunk. Pine has organically staggered drooping boughs, tapered needle sprays, varied branch lengths, dark inner volume and lighter tips, not stacked cones or disks. Real modeled 3D foliage, high quality restrained natural greens and warm chestnut bark with sculpted furrows. Tree height roughly 2.5-3 times a small neutral human scale figure standing beside them. Roots meet a short volumetric grass lawn. White or warm grey plain backdrop, whole trees uncropped, enough spacing. Lower strip contains same oak and pine in autumn (rust, ochre and muted gold) and winter (bare branching oak, snow caught along pine boughs). This is a reference board for actual 3D geometry that will be rendered in a game and then given a pixel filter, not an illustration backdrop and not a sprite sheet to paste into the level. No labels, interface, buildings, watermarks.
