## Conceito — referencia.png

Create a polished concept reference sheet for the new Lorde Goblin boss in AlgoLand, a cozy top-down farming RPG. Same character shown fully in a large three-quarter game view plus smaller front, side, back turnaround views, neutral pale background, no labels. A broad, imposing but whimsical green goblin, about 1.5x taller and much wider than a common goblin, amber eyes, long ears and tusks. Rugged asymmetric cave-forged dark iron plates, leather straps, one rough dark teal shoulder mantle, simple belt with mineral fragments. He holds a huge crude stone-headed war hammer with wrapped wood handle. Matte natural materials and hand-painted pixel art surface detail on low-poly 3D forms, warm light and subtle cool cave fill, readable silhouette at game scale. No crown, no circlet, no helmet, no headgear, no royal robe, no gems, no gold ornamentation, no glossy armor, no text, no watermark. Keep identical proportions and equipment in all views, full feet and weapon within frame, relaxed ready pose.

## Atlas inicial — atlas_materiais_v1.png

Game material texture atlas for AlgoLand stylized 3D pixel-art-painted goblin boss. Flat 2D texture painting ONLY, no objects, no perspective, no lighting render. Exact grid of FOUR columns and TWO rows of equal square swatches, edge-to-edge without margins or gutters. Landscape 2:1 canvas. Top row left to right: olive goblin skin with broad angular muted moss-green value clusters and a few restrained ochre flecks; dark charcoal forged iron with slate planar wear marks and sparse rusty edges; worn dark umber leather with a few short tan scuffs; dark desaturated petrol-teal heavy cloth with broad stepped folds. Bottom row left to right: medium dark gray-brown rough granite with large angular muted facets and sparse pale cracks; warm aged ivory with broad cream value bands; dark ochre wooden grain with broad vertical carved grain; warm dusty gray coarse binding rope with bold diagonal weave. Paint every square as a full-bleed continuous material sample, no tile frames, no separators, no labels, no lettering, no UI, no checkerboard, no alpha. Deliberate pixel art with coherent rectangular pixel clusters and crisp hard stepped edges, a restrained five-value palette within each swatch, not noisy, no gradients or anti-aliasing, no photorealism. Each swatch is designed like a native 64x64 pixel game texture visibly enlarged with nearest-neighbor. All eight swatches uniform local value, no vignette, no strong directional highlight. These textures will be UV-mapped onto actual modeled goblin anatomy, armor, cloth and hammer.

## Correção do atlas — atlas_materiais.png

Edit this exact eight-material texture atlas. Keep the precise 4-column by 2-row layout, same full-bleed swatches, same size and pixel-art medium. Correct ONLY top-row first and second swatches. First swatch goblin skin: subdued natural olive green, reduce the contrast dramatically to three very close olive values, with a mostly calm base and just a few restrained small clusters; absolutely no camouflage-like stripes or sweeping diagonal shapes, no yellow patches, no fine noise, no gradients. Second swatch dark forged iron: almost uniform matte charcoal/slate base, sparse subtle short pixel scuffs with slightly lighter edges, a very few rusty pixels; remove the broad diagonal silver bands and cracks so it does not resemble marble or striped rock. Keep every other swatch unchanged: brown leather, dark teal cloth, granite, ivory, wood, rope. Crisp deliberate chunky rectangular pixel clusters, not a filtered photograph. No text, no outlines separating swatches, no borders, no objects.
